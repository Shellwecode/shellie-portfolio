Most tab groupers sort by hostname, which means my five github tabs for
five different projects get lumped together. Tab Tender groups by the
task you opened the tab for — it watches the window you switch back to
and builds the group around that. Works well enough that I use it every
day; rough enough that I don't recommend it yet.
