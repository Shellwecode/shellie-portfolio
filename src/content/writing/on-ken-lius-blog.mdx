Liu, in one of his blog posts that introduces his new story, brings up the
idea that writing and thinking about AI is not to examine how machines can
replace humans — he instead is more interested in seeing how machines and AI
can help us be more human, more us. I guess this is why I love sci-fi,
because it is a unique lens humans produce to reflect on our own humanity.
