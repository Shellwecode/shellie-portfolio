I draw while I listen. Not the person — I'm not a courtroom sketch artist.
I draw small diagrams, arrows, icons of what they're describing. A phone
with an angry dot on it. A hand pointing at a screen. A box with a question
mark.

It does two things at once. First, it keeps me from taking notes in complete
sentences, which always tricks me into thinking I understand something
before I do. A drawing forces me to compress a thought to its shape.

Second, it gives me something to point at later. When I'm trying to explain
what a user meant, a drawing lands faster than a quote. *Here's what their
Tuesday morning looks like. Here's the thing they said would make it better.*

Drawing isn't a skill I'm showing off in these moments. It's just the
cheapest way to think with my hands.
