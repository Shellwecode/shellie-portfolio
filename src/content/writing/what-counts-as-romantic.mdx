I came across Lucy Vo's YouTube video on what she considers "a hopeless
romantic mindset." It is so interesting that she mentions nowadays people
take technologies into account for perceiving romantic moments. For
instance, never leaving someone's text unread, or liking someone's stories.
These are signs of showing interest in people, but I think that these are
not signals of romance. I also don't stand with those who immediately react
with disdain upon hearing the word "romantic." I guess it's because I
believe in romance, and that true romance comes only to ones who would
pursue and embrace it.

So, here is my list of romance / romantic moments (technology free):

- letting me be me. to be who i truly am. because i am trusted.
- cook meals for me when i am tired even tho not very good at cooking
- i guess this can extend to doing anything (not particularly good at) for me
- tender hugs and hand holdings
- grow together into self assured and confident human beings
