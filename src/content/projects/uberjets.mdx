## What I was asked to do

Virtual Hangar's booking flow felt like a form, not a trip. The ask was to
make it feel closer to buying a plane ticket — with the unique pieces (jet
selection, empty legs, operator reputation) still front and center.

## What I tried

— Cut the number of decisions on the first screen from nine to three.

— Treated the operator's reputation as social proof, not fine print.

— Mapped the whole flow as a single scroll instead of a multi-step wizard,
so people could see where they were in the journey.

## What I'd do differently

I under-weighted mobile. About 40% of inquiries came from phones, and the
single-scroll pattern didn't compress as cleanly as I'd hoped. Next time I'd
start mobile-first.
