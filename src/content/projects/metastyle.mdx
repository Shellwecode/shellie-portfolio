## The ask

MetaStyle let people generate art on their phones, but the prompting felt
like talking to a search bar. The team wanted something that felt more like
sketching.

## The approach

— Replaced the text box as the primary input with a set of visual modifiers
(style, mood, palette) you could layer and reorder.

— Kept the text field, but demoted it to an "advanced" step.

— Built the generation feedback loop around small previews — four at once,
cheap to dismiss, easy to iterate on.

## What shipped

A flow that let first-time users get to a good image in under 30 seconds,
without ever typing a full sentence.
