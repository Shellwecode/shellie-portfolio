## The premise

Amazon's shopping flow is built for power users who already know exactly what
they want. I redesigned the mobile app for the rest of us — the people who
open the app, look at three listings, get overwhelmed, and close it.

## The moves

— Surfaced the three questions I actually ask when buying online: *is it
real, is it right, will it arrive in time?*

— Demoted the filters. Most people don't filter. Most people compare.

— Added a "compare drawer" that stays pinned as you browse, so you don't lose
your spot.

## What I learned

That the hardest part of redesigning a giant app is deciding what to leave
alone. Every cut I made forced me to defend why.
